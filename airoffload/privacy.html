airoffload privacy policy

airoffload is a personal backup tool used only by its owner. It signs in to the owner's own Google account with Google OAuth and reads and writes files in that account's Google Drive, only to store and restore the owner's backups.

Data is moved directly between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. Nothing is sent to any other server, shared, sold or used for advertising, and no data is used to train AI models.

The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

airoffload's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: through the repository owner's GitHub profile, github.com/ArchiusVuong-sudo.
